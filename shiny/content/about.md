This inventory tracks peer-reviewed publications funded by and/or authored by staff of the California Department of Water Resources (DWR). It is maintained as a tool for tracking research output, identifying scientific collaborators, and reporting on departmental productivity.

**Coverage:** Publications are identified through systematic searches of the Scopus database. Each record is reviewed to confirm DWR funding or authorship before it is added to the inventory.

**Classification:** Publications are automatically classified into science fields and categories using a custom DWR taxonomy. See the **Science Fields** tab of the dashboard for the full taxonomy with field definitions.

**Data refresh:** The dataset is updated approximately every six months. The date of the most recent update is provided in the dashboard application footer. Because publications are harvested automatically from Scopus, DWR staff do not need to contribute publications through a manual submission to dataset maintainers.

For questions or to report an issue, contact [dwrscience@water.ca.gov](mailto:dwrscience@water.ca.gov).
